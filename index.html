Nextile support

Playing a round

Tap the number shown under Find next. Choose Level map for the endless journey or Free practice to repeat a board. The pause button lets you take a break.

Music and tap feedback

Open Settings from the gear on the home screen to adjust music, correct tap sounds and vibration.

Restoring your unlock

Using the Apple Account that made the purchase, open Settings, select Advanced access, then Restore purchase. If you need help, include your iOS and Nextile versions when you email the developer.
