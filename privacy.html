Nextile privacy policy

Your game data

Nextile saves your level progress, practice results and settings on this device. You can play without creating an account. Nextile does not send your scores to a server or include advertising or third-party analytics.

Purchases and previews

Apple processes purchases. Nextile checks your purchase status through the App Store to unlock the game. A record of each completed advanced preview is stored in this device’s keychain and may remain after you delete the app.

Removing data

Deleting Nextile removes its local progress, practice history and settings. Apple manages your purchase history separately.
